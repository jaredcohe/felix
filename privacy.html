Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Felix (the "App") is software built by A Place To Stand LLC ("we", "us") for our bookkeeping and accounting clients. Each client runs their own copy of the App. This policy explains how the App handles QuickBooks Online data.

What it accesses

After a client user with admin rights approves the connection through Intuit's sign-in, the client's copy of the App accesses accounting data in that client's QuickBooks Online company, including company details, customers, vendors, items, invoices, payments, bills, other transactions and financial reports.

How it is used

The data is used only for the client's own bookkeeping, invoicing, reporting and analysis, including work we do for the client as their bookkeeper. It is not sold or rented. It is not shared with third parties except as the client directs or the law requires. It is not used for advertising or to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence or machine learning models. One client's data is never combined with another's.

Where it is stored

Each copy of the App runs on computers controlled by the client or by us on the client's behalf. Access tokens and any exported data are stored there, with access limited to people the client authorizes. Data moves between those computers and Intuit over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Retention and deletion

Exported data is kept only as long as the client needs it or the law requires for accounting records, and then deleted. Disconnecting the App revokes its access and deletes its stored tokens.

Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time from inside the App or from QuickBooks Online (Settings, then Apps).
